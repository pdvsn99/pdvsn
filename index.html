work.

I'm Paul. I like working out why things don't work, and then making them work.

I'm based in Edinburgh and spend most of my time untangling complicated problems and finding better ways of doing things. At the moment that's at Octopus Energy, where I've been making it easier for people to get their gas and electricity connected.

Before that, I spent three years making videos. Different work, same approach: figure out what needs doing, get the right people together, and get it done.
